\begin{enabstract}
Academic theses often require both layout-stable PDF output and editable DOCX
output, although the two formats use different mechanisms for pagination,
styles, references, and bibliographies. The regression workflow parses
structured sources, generates PDF and DOCX, and validates both outputs. It
keeps manuscript content outside the template tool and resolves each input
from its own source directory. The case
exercises long titles, abstracts, headings, figures, tables, equations, notes,
and citations without reporting research data or empirical findings.

\enkeywords{academic document; format conversion; PDF; DOCX; quality validation}
\end{enabstract}
