\begin{enabstract}
The low-altitude economy involves standardization objects across aircraft,
infrastructure, air traffic management, safety oversight, and application
scenarios. This regression case defines a four-layer workflow covering
compliant data acquisition, text processing, topic analysis, and
standardization interpretation. Crawling records traceable public sources,
while text models generate candidate clues rather than formal standard gaps.
The case is intended to exercise long titles, abstracts, headings, figures,
tables, equations, notes, and citations in both PDF and DOCX output; it does
not claim empirical findings that have not been produced.

\enkeywords{low-altitude economy; standard demand; web crawling; text analysis; standardization engineering}
\end{enabstract}
